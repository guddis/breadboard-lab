Library

Gate symbols and truth tables

Generated by gateSym() and truthTable().

The truth tables are computed from the same JavaScript functions a simulator would use, so the table on the page cannot disagree with the circuit you describe. Edit the GATES array to change what appears here.

Library

Chip pinouts

Generated by dipSVG() from a pin table.

Every pin gets a role — IN, OUT, PWR, GND or NC — and the drawing colours itself accordingly. Outputs come out teal and bold, because wiring an output to a rail is the mistake that kills chips.

Four of the CD4000 quad-gate chips share one pin layout, so QUAD('CD40xx') builds the whole pin table for you. Chips with their own arrangement get spelled out.

When it doesn't work

Every project needs this table. It will get used.

What you seeAlmost always
Nothing lights at allPower isn't reaching pin 14 and pin 7.
A chip gets warmPower in backwards. Unplug now.
Lights flicker on their ownA floating input. Tie it to −.
An LED never lightsBackwards. Long leg toward +.
Everything is wrong at onceUnplug, walk away five minutes, re-read one table. This is what real engineers do.
The one technique worth teaching. Don't stare at the whole board. Pick one signal and follow it. Halve the problem, then halve it again.
